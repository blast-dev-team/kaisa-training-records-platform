## Brand & Style
This design system is built for a fresh new vibe on rental car companies here in Korea. 

Some of the old references are 아마존카, 다타랩, and 차즘. (these are just logical references)

What we aim to be is the 아정당 of rental car companies. We want a modern, friendly, based on #3756fc this main color. It should be a new fresh take on the old rental car industry to make it sexy.
Basically our goal is to take an unsexy business -> sexy business.
  

## Colors
  

The palette is anchored by ****Gongcar Blue (**#3756fc)****, providing a grounding, premium foundation that evokes trust and stability. 
  

- ****Primary:**** Gongcar Blue. Used for heavy typography, dark-mode sections, and primary branding.  

## Typography

  

This design system uses a dual-type approach to balance technical innovation with corporate clarity.

 

****Pretendard,sans-serif**** is used for headlines. Its geometric, slightly eccentric terminals suggest a "machine-age" precision that fits a sexy brand.
 

Use tight letter-spacing on large headlines to create a dense, modern editorial look. For body text, utilize generous line heights (1.6x) to ensure the white space feels intentional and professional.
